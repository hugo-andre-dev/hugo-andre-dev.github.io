# Support

Email **[hugo.campos@orangetribe.pt](mailto:hugo.campos@orangetribe.pt)**.

## Reporting a problem

Please include:

- your device (for example iPhone 13, Pixel 7, Steam Deck)
- its system and version (for example iOS 18, Android 14, Windows 11)
- what happened, and what you did just before

## Getting Rivet Premium back

- **iPhone and iPad:** in the game, open Settings and tap **Restore Purchases**. Use the Apple Account you bought it with.
- **Android:** Premium comes back on its own when you are signed in to Google Play with the account you bought it with.
- **Steam:** everything Premium gives is part of the Steam game, so there is nothing to restore.

## Deleting your save data

The game saves on your device and in your platform's cloud save.

1. To delete the save on your device, uninstall the game.
2. To delete the cloud copy, use your platform's settings: iCloud and Game Center on Apple devices, Google Play Games on Android, Steam Cloud on Steam.

We don't keep a copy of your save, so there is nothing for us to delete.
