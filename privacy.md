# Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

## Who we are

Rivet is published on every platform by Hugo André de Oliveira Campos: as an individual on the App Store and Google AdMob, and as a sole trader (empresário em nome individual) on Google Play and Steam. He is responsible for the data described here ("we"). Contact: [hugo.campos@orangetribe.pt](mailto:hugo.campos@orangetribe.pt).

## In short

- Rivet has no account and no analytics of its own. We don't collect or store your personal data.
- Your save stays on your device and in your platform's cloud save.
- On iPhone, iPad and Android, Google shows ads and collects some data to do so.
- The Steam version has no ads.

## Save data

The game saves your progress on your device. If you use cloud save, a copy is kept by your platform: Game Center and iCloud (Apple), Google Play Games (Google) or Steam Cloud (Valve). Each platform holds that copy under its own privacy policy. We can't see it.

## Ads (iPhone, iPad and Android)

The mobile game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to stop fraud, Google collects data from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads. See [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

- **EU, UK and Switzerland:** the game asks for your consent first. If you say no, you can still get ads, but they are not personalized. You can change your choice later in the game.
- **iPhone and iPad:** Apple's prompt asks whether the game may track you. If you say no, your advertising ID is not shared. You can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
- **Where the law requires it** (for example for players under 18, or in some countries), the game shows only ads that are not personalized.
- **Rewards never depend on your choice.** Saying no to consent or tracking doesn't change what the game gives you.
- **Rivet Premium** (a one-time purchase) removes the ads.

## Age checks

Where the law requires an age check, the game uses the age signal your platform provides (Apple's Declared Age Range or Google Play's Age Signals). Where the platform gives none, the game asks you for your age range.

Your age range stays on your device. It is used only to decide which ads and features are allowed. Google's age signal is never used for ads.

## Purchases

Rivet Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play, and the Steam game through Steam. They handle the payment. We never see your payment details; the game only learns whether you own Premium.

## What we see

The stores give every publisher summary numbers, such as downloads and sales. These don't identify you.

## Email

If you email us, we get your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you, and keep it only as long as that takes.

## Children

Rivet is made for teens and adults and is not aimed at children.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask to see, correct, move or delete your data, object to how it's used, withdraw consent, and complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Apart from support emails, we hold no personal data, so most requests go to the platform that holds it:

- Apple: [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- Google (Google Play, Google Play Games and AdMob): [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- Valve (Steam): [Steam's privacy policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/)

For anything else, email us.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we update it here and change the date at the top.
